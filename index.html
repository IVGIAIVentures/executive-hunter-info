Executive Hunter TEST

Executive Hunter is a personal job-search assistant for its account owner. It runs on the owner's Windows computer, finds public management job opportunities, reads selected job alerts in Gmail, and saves results to a dedicated Google Sheets test spreadsheet.

The Google consent screen currently identifies the app as Executive Hunter TEST — Gmail Readonly. Its separate, optional Gmail sending connection supports applications reviewed and approved by the owner.

Why Google access is requested

  • Gmail read-only: read job alerts and extract job titles and public source links. The Google permission can read the mailbox; application filters limit normal retrieval to job alerts.
  • Account email: verify the intended Google account.
  • Optional Gmail sending: send one reviewed application with the selected CV only after the owner's explicit confirmation. Searching never authorizes sending.

There is no public sign-up, applicant database, or service for other users. This information website does not run the assistant or receive CV uploads, mailbox content, or Google credentials.
