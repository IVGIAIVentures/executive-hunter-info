Executive Hunter TEST

Personal-use terms

Executive Hunter TEST is a private experimental desktop tool for its account owner's job search. It is not an employment agency, a public recruiting platform or a guarantee of employment.

Review of results

A search result is not proof that a vacancy is open or that the owner satisfies its requirements. The owner reviews the source, date, employer and requirements before relying on a record. The tool distinguishes advertised jobs from possible opportunities and target companies.

Applications and permissions

The owner controls Google permissions and may revoke access. No application or recruiter message may be sent without explicit approval for the displayed opportunity, recipient, text and CV. Enabling a search schedule does not authorize sending. TEST scheduling and the central sending switch are currently off.

Sources and cost

The tool uses permitted public search results, supported APIs and authorized job-alert access. It does not bypass logins, private-group controls, platform restrictions or security warnings. Free quotas and service availability can change. The intended service cost is close to zero within existing free allowances; electricity, internet and existing subscriptions are separate. A purchase or paid upgrade requires the owner's approval.

Local operation

The computer, network and valid account permissions are required for local work. The tool can fail or miss records, and its output must remain reviewable. The owner maintains private backups and controls retention. This information website does not host the personal application or accept CV uploads.
